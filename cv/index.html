Profile

  • I am a second-year PhD candidate at the Information Engineering Lab (ieLab), The University of Queensland, supervised by Dr. Teerapong Leelanupab and Prof. Guido Zuccon. My research focuses on optimising retrieval for deep research agents: both building retrieval systems that better serve agents and helping agents make better use of retrieval. I am also interested in LLM-based ranking and retrieval-augmented generation.
